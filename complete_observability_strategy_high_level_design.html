Enterprise End-to-End Observability Strategy

A federated observability architecture for decentralized data platforms, data products and MLOps workloads, integrating Databricks, AWS data/integration services, Airflow, OpenTelemetry and OpenLineage. The target state combines real-time monitoring with cross-platform correlation while allowing domains to build their own Gold observability products.

Federated by designMulti-tenantStreaming-firstOpen standardsLevel 1 MonitoringLevel 2 CorrelationMedallion observability lakehouse

1. North Star

North Star: Create one correlated observability fabric across data, pipelines, services, platforms, MLOps and cost so that a support or engineering team can move from symptom to impacted data product to probable technical cause with the same shared context.
SeeContinuous visibility into health, quality, performance, capacity and cost.
ConnectLink datasets, jobs, services, infrastructure, tenants and spend.
ActGive L1/L2 and engineering teams actionable context for alerting, triage and RCA.

3. Architecture Principles

PrincipleDecision
Observability is dataTelemetry and operational metadata are governed enterprise data products.
Federated, not fragmentedCentralize standards, collection, canonical Silver, topology and governance; federate Gold use cases.
Streaming-first, not streaming-onlyUse streaming for runtime telemetry; pull/micro-batch for control-plane and billing data.
Transport is not storageKinesis/MSK moves events; Bronze provides durable replayable history.
Open standards firstOpenTelemetry for service telemetry; OpenLineage for job/run/dataset context.
Canonical identities before dashboardsEntity IDs and relationships are foundational for Level 2 correlation.
Tenant-aware from ingestion onwardTenant/domain/product identity is attached, resolved and enforced through the lifecycle.
Operational and analytical paths are separateCritical alerting should not wait for long analytical transformations.
Retain by valueSignal, criticality, environment, compliance and analytical value drive retention.
Platform observes itselfCollector health, lag, drops, mapping quality and completeness are platform SLOs.

Rule: push when the producer knows an event happened; pull when the authoritative system owns a state that must be queried. Do not force real-time semantics onto sources whose authoritative records arrive hourly or daily.

15. Security, Governance & Observability-Data Quality

Security & governance

  • Private connectivity where required
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • IAM/service identities and least privilege
  • AWS Secrets Manager for credentials
  • Unity Catalog for observability datasets
  • Raw-log restrictions and field masking
  • Schema contracts and producer ownership
  • Environment/tenant segregation
  • Retention/legal-hold controls
  • Access auditing

Observe the observability platform

  • collection completeness
  • source freshness / ingestion lag
  • event drop/retry rate
  • duplicate rate
  • schema-conformance rate
  • tenant-resolution coverage
  • entity-resolution coverage
  • correlation coverage
  • stream-processing lag
  • Bronze-to-Silver reconciliation

16. Non-Functional Requirements

AreaRequirement
AvailabilityCollection failures must not block source workloads; buffering and graceful degradation required.
DurabilityBronze must be replayable and independently recoverable.
ScalabilityScale collection, transport, processing, storage and serving independently.
LatencyDifferentiated T0/T1/T2/T3 telemetry classes instead of universal real-time SLA.
SecurityTenant-aware least privilege and sensitive-data controls.
CostSampling, aggregation, storage lifecycle and retention by value.
ResilienceRetry, DLQ, checkpoints, replay, late-event processing and backfill.
InteroperabilityOpen standards at collection boundaries and canonical contracts in Silver.
ExtensibilityNew telemetry sources without replatforming.

20. Architecture Decisions / Items to Lock Down

DecisionRecommended direction
Primary stream backboneSelect Kinesis or MSK as enterprise default; avoid dual-backbone complexity unless justified.
Bronze storageS3 + Delta; preserve original payload and replayability.
Canonical modelVersion before broad Level 2 onboarding.
Tenant registryOne authoritative mapping service/table with temporal history.
Gold ownershipShared Gold centrally owned; domain Gold federated.
Hot-path UXReuse enterprise monitoring/ITSM stack instead of rebuilding incident UX in the lakehouse.
RetentionPolicy-based by signal/tier/environment; do not inherit source-tool retention as enterprise policy.
CriticalityDefine T1/T2/T3 to drive monitoring depth, SLO, retention and support response.
